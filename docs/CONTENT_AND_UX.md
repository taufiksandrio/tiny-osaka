# Content & UX Design Contract

Positioning: expressive Osaka-inspired Japanese street-food. Primary CTA converts to verified GoFood/GrabFood product link, fallback store; sticky mobile CTA when appropriate. Onsite checkout excluded. Authentic Tiny Osaka logo and user photographs are authoritative. Other Japanese brands are moodboard inspiration, never designs/images to copy.

Public templates proposed: Home (hero, top menu, promo, about, CTA); About (story, proof, delivery-only); Food Guide (hero, educational narrative, image/video, product cross-link); Category/Menu (filter/list, prices, primary CTA); Product detail (photo/video, bilingual copy, modifier explanation, platform buttons); Promotion (terms, start/end dates, CTA); Location (single Bandung site, address only after verified, delivery-only disclaimer).

CMS field schema should have localized title, teaser, body, SEO title/description, OG image, slug; owner approval per revision; fixed templates and rich-text areas, not page builder; media embed manual. Do not make up ingredient, health, authenticity, branch, discount, citywide delivery, price, rating, promotional validity or opening-hour claims.

Food photo retouch outside CMS: preserve genuine portion, ingredients, product presentation, toppings and perceived size; lighting, background, color grade and surrounding props may be adjusted but must not misrepresent served food.

Accessibility: keyboard nav, meaningful alt, contrast, semantic headings, focus indicators, avoid autoplay audio and motion effects that interfere with UX. Content should be natural localized Indonesian and English, not direct literal translation.

Measurement: identify CTA placements and events consistent with `docs/DATA_CONTRACTS.md`. User arriving from ChatGPT Ads should immediately see relevant offering and merchant CTA; don't imply advertiser benefits guarantee organic AI recommendation.
